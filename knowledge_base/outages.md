# Power Outages

## How do I report an outage?
Report an outage with your customer ID and area. If an outage ticket already exists for your area, you are added to it automatically and notified on restoration.

## What do outage statuses mean?
- **reported**: the outage is logged and a crew is being assigned.
- **in_progress**: a crew is on site, and an estimated restoration time is available.
- **resolved**: power has been restored. Report again if your supply is still down.

## Planned maintenance
Planned maintenance is announced at least 24 hours in advance. Estimated restoration times for planned work are usually accurate to within an hour.

## Safety
Never touch fallen lines. Keep at least 10 metres away and report them immediately as an emergency.
