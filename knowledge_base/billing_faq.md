# Billing FAQ

## How is my bill calculated?
Bills use a slab tariff. Each block of units is charged at its own rate, so higher usage pushes the last units into a more expensive slab. A fixed monthly charge is added, and tax is applied to the energy charge plus the fixed charge.

## Why did my bill go up when my usage only rose a little?
Once usage crosses a slab boundary, the extra units are billed at the higher slab rate. Crossing from 200 to 270 units, for example, moves those 70 units into the top slab.

## When is the bill due?
Bills are due 15 days after the bill date. A late fee of 1.25% per month applies to unpaid amounts after the due date.

## How do I dispute a bill?
Raise a billing dispute within 30 days of the bill date with your customer ID and the meter reading you believe is correct. A meter inspection may be scheduled.
