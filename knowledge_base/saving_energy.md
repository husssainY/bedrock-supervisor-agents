# Saving Energy

## Why is my usage higher in summer?
Cooling drives most summer increases. One 1.5-ton air conditioner running 8 hours a day can add 150 to 250 units a month, depending on its star rating and thermostat setting.

## Quick wins
- Set air conditioners to 24 to 26 °C. Each degree lower adds roughly 6% to cooling energy.
- Replace old ceiling fans with BLDC fans, which use about half the power.
- Run washing machines and water heaters on timers rather than leaving them on.
- Switch off appliances at the wall. Standby loads can add 5 to 10 units a month.

## Solar and net metering
Rooftop solar customers on net metering are billed only for net imported units. Exported units are credited at the feed-in rate on the next bill.
